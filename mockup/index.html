Kumiho

A messenger with no server you have to trust.

  • Messages stay readable only to you and the person you send them to, even against a future quantum computer.
  • No phone number, email or username. You add people face to face by scanning their Glyph.
  • You choose, per contact, how much of your metadata to hide.

Mixed into your storage key. Required on Linux computers without a TPM.
